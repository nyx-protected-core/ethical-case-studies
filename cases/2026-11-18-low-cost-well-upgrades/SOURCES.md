# Sources: low-cost well and handpump upgrades

Status date: 18 November 2026.

## Method

- **Institutional reports and manuals:** each PDF or official page was fetched and the cited figures were found in the text.
- **Peer-reviewed papers:** where an open PDF or author/institutional copy was available, that text was read. Where only a journal landing page or abstract was open, the source is labelled accordingly. DOIs are given as public identifiers.
- **Working papers** are labelled as such and are not treated as journal articles.
- No figure was taken from memory or from search-engine summaries alone.

## Notes on numbers

- The 2009 RWSN "one in three" figure and the 2020 "one in four" figure describe overlapping but not identical country sets and methods. Both are reported, with their dates.
- JMP 2023 gender figures ([S4], [S5]) use data up to 2022. JMP 2025 coverage figures ([S1]) use data through 2024. They are not combined into a single arithmetic claim.
- Monetary figures are left in the price-years of the sources (2011, 2013, 2014, 2018) and are not inflated to 2026.
- "**(our arithmetic)**" in the case study is used only for the comparison of US$2,823 with US$10,487 in [S28] (rehabilitation costing about one quarter as much as a new borehole).
