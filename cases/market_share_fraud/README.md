# Market share Fraud

Date: 2026-11-19

*The category name is the repository's. It does not label every case as fraud: patent and copyright cases are described as infringement, and "theft" is used only where a court, regulator or plea found theft or misappropriation.*

*Category overview. Research and writing only; not legal advice. Sources were checked on 19 November 2026.*

## What this category covers

Cases where a company was found, by a court, a jury, a regulator or its own plea, to have taken or infringed another firm's technology or rights, or to have used fraud, bribery or collusion, in a contest whose real prize was a market position. The recurring question is whether the penalty, when it finally came, was small next to the market the conduct helped win.

**Inclusion rule.** A company gets its own case only if there is an adjudicated or admitted finding against it: a conviction, a guilty plea, a deferred-prosecution or remediation agreement, a final civil judgment of theft, fraud, misappropriation or infringement, or a regulator's sanction. Filed or pending suits, charges and settlements without findings are listed below under *Alleged, not adjudicated*, not as cases. Patent and copyright infringement are reported as infringement; the word "theft" is used only where a finding of trade-secret theft or misappropriation exists.

**How figures are treated.** Damages are compared with market effect only where a court or official record gives a figure. Government and prosecution figures are flagged for reporting incentives. No case in this category estimates a market share that no record measured.

**Not used here.** This category is global. It uses no Canadian data, institutions or studies.

## Repeat offenders

Companies with more than one adjudicated finding in the records opened so far:

- **Samsung Electronics.** Two separate Apple suits each ended in a jury finding of patent infringement that survived appeal: the 2011 suit (2012 and 2018 verdicts) and the 2012 suit ($119,625,000 judgment reinstated en banc in 2016). In the second suit the jury also found that Apple infringed a Samsung patent. Patent infringement does not require intent and is not described here as theft. Records: https://www.cafc.uscourts.gov/opinions-orders/14-1335.opinion.5-14-2015.1.pdf ; https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/cafc/15-1171/15-1171-2016-10-07.html
- **Hytera Communications.** A civil judgment (trade secrets and copyright) and a criminal guilty plea, for the same course of conduct, in two separate forums. Records: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ca7-22-02370/pdf/USCOURTS-ca7-22-02370-0.pdf ; https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndil/media/1384221/dl?inline=
- **Rimini Street.** Copyright infringement found and affirmed in Oracle's first suit (2018), then civil contempt of the resulting injunction on five issues (2022). Findings in Oracle's second suit were mostly vacated on appeal and the suit was settled in 2025 with no admission of liability. Records: https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2018/01/08/16-16832.pdf ; https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nvd.71326/gov.uscourts.nvd.71326.1548.0.pdf
- **Apple.** Three separate adjudicated findings in different matters: patent infringement found by the ITC (Masimo), a final patent-infringement judgment (VirnetX), and an unfair-competition ruling followed by wilful civil contempt (Epic Games, not intellectual property). Records: https://www.cafc.uscourts.gov/opinions-orders/24-1285.OPINION.3-19-2026_2663002.pdf ; https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.txed.124653/gov.uscourts.txed.124653.1107.0.pdf ; https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2025/12/11/25-2935.pdf

## How to read a case in this category

Each case states the finding and its record, a timeline, the damages or penalties against any measured market effect, an analysis grounded in peer-reviewed work on deterrence and innovation, fair counterarguments (including legitimate intellectual-property protection and the costs of litigation), proposed solutions, what the case does not replace, and the items that remain unverified.

## Note on hardware

Several cases concern physical products (radios, memory chips, wind turbines, fibres, wheels, batteries, phones, a smartwatch sensor, racing cars). None is a hardware design study: no case contains a design, a parts list or build instructions. Each is a legal and ethical analysis of a dispute about such products.
