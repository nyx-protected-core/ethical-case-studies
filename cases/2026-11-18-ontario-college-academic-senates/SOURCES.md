# Sources: academic senates for Ontario's public colleges

Status date: 18 November 2026.

## Method

- **Statutes, regulations and directives:** we opened the official consolidation or the government PDF and found the cited provision in the text.
- **College, employer and union documents:** we fetched each PDF or page and found the quoted words in the text. Union and employer documents are advocacy sources and are labelled as such.
- **Court decisions:** we read the trial decision in full PDF. The appellate decision is cited from a third-party summary only and is marked unverified in the README.
- **Peer-reviewed papers:** we checked each DOI's metadata (authors, year, journal, volume, pages) against OpenAlex or Crossref. Findings come from the published abstract unless the text says otherwise. Where a working paper and a journal article exist, both are listed and the version actually read is named.
- No figure was taken from memory or from search-engine summaries alone. Two DOIs found during research were wrong and are not cited (see the end of this file).
