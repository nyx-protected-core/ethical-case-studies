# Sources: full-time faculty in Ontario's public colleges

Status date: 18 November 2026.

## Method

- **Collective agreement, arbitration award and taskforce report:** we fetched each PDF (or opened its text through a web reader where direct download failed) and found the cited clause or figure in the text.
- **Union, employer and sector reports:** fetched and searched. They are advocacy sources and are labelled as such. Their figures are reported as their figures.
- **News:** used only for events and figures attributed to named organizations. Students and individual workers quoted in news are not named.
- **Peer-reviewed papers:** we checked each DOI's metadata (authors, year, journal, volume, pages) against OpenAlex or Crossref. Findings come from the published abstract unless the note says the full text was read. Where an online-first year and an issue year differ, both are given.
- No figure was taken from memory or from search-engine summaries alone.
