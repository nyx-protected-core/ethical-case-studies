# Sources

All sources were checked on 18 November 2026.

**How sources were verified**

- **DOIs:** each DOI was resolved through doi.org (HTTP 302 redirect to the publisher) and its
  metadata (title, authors, journal, year, volume, pages) was confirmed through the Crossref API.
  The bibliographic details below were generated from that Crossref record.
- **Claims:** every figure used in the case study was checked against the paper's abstract, read
  through OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, the publisher page, PubMed Central, ERIC or arXiv.
- **Links:** fetched directly. Where a site blocked automated fetching, that is noted.
- **Status values:** VERIFIED (exists and supports the stated claim), PARTLY VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED
  (not relied on).
