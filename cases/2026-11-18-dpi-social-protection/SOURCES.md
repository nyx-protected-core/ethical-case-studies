# Sources

All sources were checked on 18 November 2026.

**How DOIs were checked:** each DOI was resolved at doi.org, where it had to return an HTTP redirect, and its metadata was matched against the Crossref API. The abstract was then read through OpenAlex, Europe PMC or the publisher before any figure from it was used.

**How links were checked:** each link was fetched, and its text was checked for the specific claim cited.

**Status labels:**

- **VERIFIED:** the DOI resolves, the Crossref metadata matches and the claim was found in the abstract or text, or the link was fetched and the claim was found on the page.
- **PARTLY VERIFIED:** the page was reached, but only part of the cited content could be confirmed.
- **UNVERIFIED / DROPPED:** the source could not be confirmed, so nothing in the case study rests on it.
