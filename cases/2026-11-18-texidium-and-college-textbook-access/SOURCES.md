# Sources: Texidium and college textbook access

Status date: 18 November 2026.

## Method

- **Platform and college pages:** we opened each page and found the quoted words in the text. Pages can change. What is reported is what each page said when opened for this study.
- **Statutes, directives and court decisions:** we opened the official text or a public decision that quotes it, and found the cited passage.
- **News and student newspapers:** used for events and statements attributed to organizations. Individual students, faculty and staff quoted are not named.
- **Peer-reviewed papers:** we checked each DOI's metadata (authors, year, journal, volume, pages) against OpenAlex or Crossref. Findings come from the published abstract, unless the note says otherwise.
- No figure was taken from memory or from search-engine summaries alone, except where marked (S37).
