# Sources: Living Wages in Global Supply Chains

*All checks made 18 November 2026.*

*Revisions (18 November 2026).* Revised after an independent review. The changes correct facts, fill gaps and tighten wording. The review is not named here.

## Method

- **DOIs:** each was resolved through doi.org (HTTP 302 to the publisher) and matched against Crossref metadata (authors, title, venue, volume, pages). Findings were taken only from abstracts read through Crossref, OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar, or from a fetched full text.
- **Web pages and PDFs:** each was fetched and the cited passage found in the fetched text. Where an automated fetch was blocked, the same page was read with a second fetch method.
- **Counts:** 53 sources cited; 52 verified; 1 partly verified (S11, a news report of an official gazette).
- **Unverified items in the text, each labelled where it appears:**
  - later Bangladesh minimum-wage increments;
  - the ILO implementation report to the 355th Session;
  - an India tea living-wage figure;
  - any figure on wage-driven buyer relocation.
- **Attributed, not independently confirmed:**
  - the ACT factory and worker counts (attributed to the Clean Clothes Campaign);
  - the Fair Food Program figures (self-reported).
