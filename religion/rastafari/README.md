# Rastafari

Links only. No scripture text is copied here.

Rastafari has no single exclusive scripture. The Hebrew Bible and the Christian New Testament, often in the King James Version, are widely read. Some communities also read Ethiopian and early Rastafari books, including the Kebra Nagast and the Holy Piby. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.

## Original languages and traditional texts

- The Greek New Testament and the Hebrew Bible are listed under [Christianity](../christianity/README.md) and [Judaism — Torah](../judaism/torah/README.md), with their own publishers and licences.
- [The Kebra Nagast on Sacred Texts](https://sacred-texts.com/chr/kn/index.htm): a public-domain English edition of this Ethiopian work, as hosted by the Archive. Publisher: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)).
- [The Holy Piby on Sacred Texts](https://sacred-texts.com/afr/piby/index.htm): a public-domain edition of this early Rastafari text, as hosted by the Archive. Publisher: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)).
