# Norse paganism and modern Heathenry — the Eddas

Links only. No scripture text is copied here. Historical Norse religion was not based on a single closed canon. The Poetic Edda and the Prose Edda, in Old Norse, are the principal medieval written collections used by modern Heathen and related pagan groups. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.
