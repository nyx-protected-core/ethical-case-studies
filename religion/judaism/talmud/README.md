# Talmud

Links only. No scripture text is copied here. This page covers the Mishnah, the Babylonian Talmud (Bavli), and the Jerusalem Talmud (Yerushalmi). The main original languages are Hebrew and Aramaic. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.

## Original languages (Hebrew and Aramaic)

### Babylonian Talmud (Bavli)

- [Sefaria, Babylonian Talmud](https://www.sefaria.org/texts/Talmud/Bavli): the complete Bavli in the original languages, beginning from the tractate index. A single tractate, for example, is at [Berakhot](https://www.sefaria.org/Berakhot). Publisher: Sefaria. Site terms: [Sefaria Terms of Use](https://www.sefaria.org/terms).
- [Mechon Mamre, Talmud Bavli](https://www.mechon-mamre.org/b/l/l0.htm): the Babylonian Talmud in Hebrew and Aramaic. Publisher: Mechon Mamre. Licence: copyrighted; the publisher states that the material may be used only for private study and teaching, and expressly invites links to its text files ([copying and fair use](https://mechon-mamre.org/copy.htm)).
- [Hebrew Wikisource, Talmud Bavli](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%AA%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%93_%D7%91%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%99): the Bavli in Hebrew and Aramaic on Wikisource. Publisher: Wikisource. Licence: Wikisource hosts public-domain works and states that contributions to the site are under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike ([copyright policy](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Wikisource:Copyright_policy); [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)).

### Jerusalem Talmud (Yerushalmi)

- [Sefaria, Jerusalem Talmud](https://www.sefaria.org/texts/Talmud/Yerushalmi): the Yerushalmi in the original languages. Publisher: Sefaria. Site terms: [Sefaria Terms of Use](https://www.sefaria.org/terms).
- [Mechon Mamre, Talmud Yerushalmi](https://www.mechon-mamre.org/b/r/r0.htm): the Jerusalem Talmud in Hebrew and Aramaic. Publisher: Mechon Mamre. Licence: copyrighted; the publisher states that the material may be used only for private study and teaching, and expressly invites links to its text files ([copying and fair use](https://mechon-mamre.org/copy.htm)).

### Al HaTorah

- [Al HaTorah](https://alhatorah.org/): a free study site for Tanakh, Mishnah and Talmud in the original languages, with selectable commentaries. Publisher: Al HaTorah. Terms: terms not stated on a dedicated licence page found the home page is free to read with no sign-in. Separate Shas and Yerushalmi addresses were not confirmed, so they are not listed as separate entries here.

## English

- [The William Davidson Talmud on Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org/william-davidson-talmud): a complete English (and Modern Hebrew) edition of the Babylonian Talmud, read next to the original on Sefaria, for example at [Berakhot](https://www.sefaria.org/Berakhot). Publisher: Sefaria, hosting the William Davidson edition. Licence: the William Davidson edition's English and Modern Hebrew translations are released under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial licence, as stated on the edition page ([edition](https://www.sefaria.org/william-davidson-talmud); [CC BY-NC](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)); [Sefaria Terms of Use](https://www.sefaria.org/terms). This edition covers the Babylonian Talmud, not the Jerusalem Talmud.
- [Rodkinson English Babylonian Talmud on Sacred Texts](https://sacred-texts.com/jud/talmud.htm): a public-domain English translation. It covers only part of the Babylonian Talmud (the publisher describes it as about one third, with the Festivals and Jurisprudence sections complete). Publisher of the edition, as the site names it: the 1918 Rodkinson edition. Host: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: public domain, as stated on Sacred Texts' edition page and on Wikisource; this English edition covers only part of the Babylonian Talmud ([Sacred Texts copyrights](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights); Wikisource hosts public-domain works and states that contributions to the site are under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike ([copyright policy](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Wikisource:Copyright_policy); [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/))).
- [Rodkinson English Babylonian Talmud on Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Talmud_(Rodkinson)): the same incomplete public-domain English edition. Host: Wikisource. Licence: Wikisource hosts public-domain works and states that contributions to the site are under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike ([copyright policy](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Wikisource:Copyright_policy); [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)).

A complete, clearly licensed free English Jerusalem Talmud was not confirmed, so none is listed here. The original-language Yerushalmi links are above.
