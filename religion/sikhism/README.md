# Sikhism — Guru Granth Sahib

Links only. No scripture text is copied here. The central scripture is the Guru Granth Sahib, whose original language is a sacred Sikh register of Punjabi and related North Indian languages, written in Gurmukhi. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.

## Original language (Gurmukhi)

- [SearchGurbani, Guru Granth Sahib, Ang 1](https://www.searchgurbani.com/guru-granth-sahib/ang/1): free online reading of the scripture from the first ang, with navigation through the whole work. Publisher: SearchGurbani.com. Licence: the reader pages display the notice "All rights reserved"; a dedicated terms-of-use page was not found (the site's /about address returned 404). Terms: "All rights reserved" as stated on the reader, otherwise terms not stated. The text is free to read with no sign-in..
- [SriGranth.org reader](https://www.srigranth.org/servlet/gurbani.gurbani): another free online reader for the Guru Granth Sahib. Publisher: SriGranth.org. Terms: terms not stated on the reader page. The page is free to use with no sign-in.
