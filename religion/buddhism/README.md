# Buddhism

Links only. No scripture text is copied here. Theravada collections are centred on the Pali Canon (Tipitaka). Mahayana collections include many sutras in Sanskrit, Chinese, Tibetan and other languages. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.

## Original languages (Pali and other canonical languages)

- [SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net/): early Buddhist texts from the Tipitaka, in Pali and in parallel canonical languages, free to read with no sign-in. Publisher: SuttaCentral. Licence: the site is free to read with no sign-in; licence information for individual texts is published on the project's [licensing page](https://suttacentral.net/licensing). That page is a scripted application page, so the exact wording is not quoted from static HTML here. Prefer the Sacred Texts and Access to Insight links below where a public-domain or clearly stated licence is available..
- [Access to Insight, Tipitaka](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/index.html): a free Theravada collection of Pali Canon material with English, and with references to the Pali. Publisher: Access to Insight, with the domain owned by the Barre Center for Buddhist Studies, as the site states. Licence: the site states that everything on it is offered in cooperation with the authors and publishers, with the clear understanding that none of it is to be sold, and invites readers to help themselves to whatever they find useful; further copyright detail is on the FAQ ([FAQ, including copyright](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/faq.html#copyright); a [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) link also appears on that page).
- [Sacred Texts, Buddhism](https://sacred-texts.com/bud/index.htm): public-domain Buddhist scriptures and translations, including Pali Canon material and some Mahayana sutras. Publisher: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)).
- [GRETIL](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html): scholarly electronic texts, including Buddhist literature in Sanskrit, Pali and other languages. Publisher: GRETIL, University of Göttingen. Licence: terms not stated on the introduction page ([GRETIL home](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html)).
