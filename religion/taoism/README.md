# Taoism — Tao Te Ching and Zhuangzi

Links only. No scripture text is copied here. The best-known early Taoist books include the Tao Te Ching (Daodejing) and the Zhuangzi, in Classical Chinese. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.

## Original language (Classical Chinese)

- [Chinese Wikisource, Daodejing](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E9%81%93%E5%BE%B7%E7%B6%93): the Tao Te Ching in Chinese. Publisher: Wikisource. Licence: Wikisource hosts public-domain works and states that contributions to the site are under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike ([copyright policy](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Wikisource:Copyright_policy); [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)).
- [Sacred Texts, Taoism](https://sacred-texts.com/tao/index.htm): Classical Chinese Taoist texts in transcription and in translation, including the Tao Te Ching and the Zhuangzi. Publisher: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)).

The Chinese Text Project is a major reading site for these works, but its terms page and text pages did not return a usable unchallenged copy, so they are not relied on here.
