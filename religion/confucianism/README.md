# Confucianism — the Analects and the Four Books and Five Classics

Links only. No scripture text is copied here. The core Confucian books are commonly grouped as the Four Books and the Five Classics, including the Analects, in Classical Chinese. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.

## Original language (Classical Chinese)

- [Sacred Texts, Confucianism](https://sacred-texts.com/cfu/index.htm): the Confucian classics in Chinese transcription and in translation, including the Analects and the wider Four Books and Five Classics as hosted there. Publisher: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)).

The Chinese Text Project is a major reading site for these works, but its terms page and text pages did not return a usable unchallenged copy, so they are not relied on here.
