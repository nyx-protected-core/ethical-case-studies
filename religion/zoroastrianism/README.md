# Zoroastrianism — Avesta

Links only. No scripture text is copied here. The Avesta is the principal corpus of Zoroastrian scripture, in Avestan, with later Middle Persian (Pahlavi) literature. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.

## Original language (Avestan)

- [Avesta.org, Zoroastrian Archives](https://www.avesta.org/): a long-standing free archive of Avestan texts and related literature, including a [Yasna](https://www.avesta.org/yasna/index.html) section. Publisher: Avesta.org (Zoroastrian Archives). A dedicated site-wide licence page at /copyright.html was not found (that address returned 404). Terms: terms not stated as a single licence; some items on the site carry their own copyright notices. The pages are free to read with no sign-in. Prefer the Sacred Texts links below when a clearly stated public-domain or site-wide licence is needed.

## English

- [Sacred Texts, Zoroastrianism](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/index.htm): public-domain English translations of Avestan texts, as hosted by the Archive. Publisher: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)).
- [Avesta.org](https://www.avesta.org/): English translations and tools next to the original-language materials. Publisher: Avesta.org. Terms: terms not stated as a single licence (see above).

## Other languages

Some European-language public-domain translations appear in the [Sacred Texts Zoroastrian collection](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/index.htm). Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)). Avesta.org also hosts materials in more than one modern language; those should be read under any notice attached to the individual item, because no single site-wide licence page was found.
