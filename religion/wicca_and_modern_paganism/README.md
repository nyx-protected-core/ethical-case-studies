# Wicca and modern paganism

Links only. No scripture text is copied here.

Wicca and most modern pagan paths have no single, shared, official scripture. A personal Book of Shadows is common in Wicca, but that is a private or coven text, not a public canon. Some modern groups use older mythological or occult books; those are not a Wiccan or pagan "bible" unless a given group treats them that way.

- [Sacred Texts, Paganism and related collections](https://sacred-texts.com/pag/index.htm): a free archive of older public-domain texts that some modern readers consult. This is a historical collection, not an official Wiccan or pagan scripture. Publisher: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)).

No official Wiccan scripture link is listed, because no such single public scripture was found.
