# Yazidism

Links only. No scripture text is copied here.

Yazidi religious tradition is carried especially in sacred hymns (qewls) and in oral teaching. There is no single, universally accepted, official written scripture comparable to a closed canon. Two Arabic books sometimes circulated under Yazidi names have been widely disputed as to authenticity, and they are not listed here as official scripture.

No official, complete, free collection of the hymns with a confirmed public licence was found. A page of secondary older material about the tradition exists at the Internet Sacred Text Archive, but that is not the community's scripture and is not presented as such.

There is therefore no scripture link on this page.
