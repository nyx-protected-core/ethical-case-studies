# Druze faith

Links only. No scripture text is copied here.

The Druze community's principal religious writings are traditionally known as the Epistles of Wisdom (Rasa'il al-Hikma), in Arabic. Those writings are, by the community's own practice, not generally published in full for public reading. No official, complete, free public edition with stated terms was found, and none is listed here.

There is therefore no original-language, English, or other-language scripture link on this page. That is a fact about what is published, not a substitute text.
