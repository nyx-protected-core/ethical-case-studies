# Samaritanism

Links only. No scripture text is copied here.

The central Samaritan scripture is the Samaritan Torah (Samaritan Pentateuch), in the Samaritan Hebrew tradition. A complete, free online edition with a confirmed public licence or terms page, and with no sign-in, was not found (a Wikisource title at the expected English address was not available).

There is therefore no scripture link on this page. That is a fact about what could be confirmed as free to use, not a substitute text. The Jewish Torah links under [Judaism — Torah](../judaism/torah/README.md) are a different textual tradition and should not be treated as the Samaritan text.
