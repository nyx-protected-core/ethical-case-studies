# Indigenous religions of the Americas, Australia and the Pacific

Links only. No scripture text is copied here.

Indigenous religions of the Americas, Australia and the Pacific are many, not one. They are carried in large part by oral tradition, ceremony, law and community teaching. There is no single shared written scripture for these regions.

Some older collections of narratives, chants and ethnographic texts have been published and later placed in public-domain archives. Those collections are not a substitute for living oral tradition, and they should not be treated as a single Indigenous "canon." Where a community has asked that a text not be circulated, that wish should be respected; nothing of that kind is listed here.

- [Sacred Texts, Native American collections](https://sacred-texts.com/nam/index.htm): a free archive of older published collections from parts of the Americas. Publisher: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)).
- Related older Pacific and other regional collections may be reached from the [Sacred Texts home](https://sacred-texts.com/). Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)).

No official, pan-Indigenous scripture is listed, because none exists.
