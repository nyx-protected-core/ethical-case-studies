# Hinduism

Links only. No scripture text is copied here. The texts most often treated as foundational include the Vedas, the Upanishads, and the Bhagavad Gita (a part of the Mahabharata). Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.

## Original language (Sanskrit)

- [Sacred Texts, Hinduism index](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/index.htm): a free collection of Sanskrit-related Hindu scriptures in transcription and in translation. Publisher: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)). Sub-collections include the [Rig Veda](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/rigveda/index.htm), the [Upanishads](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/upan/index.htm), and the [Bhagavad Gita](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/gita/index.htm).
- [GRETIL, Göttingen Register of Electronic Texts in Indian Languages](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html): a scholarly register of electronic texts in Sanskrit and related languages, including Vedic, Upanishadic and epic literature. Publisher: GRETIL, University of Göttingen. Licence: terms not stated on the introduction page ([GRETIL home](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html)). Prefer the Sacred Texts links when a clearly stated public-domain or site-wide licence is needed.

## Other languages

No complete, clearly licensed set of Vedas, Upanishads and Gita in a wide range of modern languages was confirmed on a single free hub with stated terms. Some vernacular and later-language texts appear in GRETIL (licence: terms not stated on the introduction page ([GRETIL home](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html))) and on language-specific Wikisource projects (licence: Wikisource hosts public-domain works and states that contributions to the site are under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike ([copyright policy](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Wikisource:Copyright_policy); [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/))). Those should be opened on their own project pages, where each work's licence is shown.
