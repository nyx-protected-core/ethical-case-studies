# Christianity — New Testament

Links only. No scripture text is copied here.

No original New Testament autograph manuscripts survive. The Greek texts below are later critical editions compiled from surviving manuscripts, or images of an early manuscript. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.

## Original language (Greek)

- [Nestle 1904 Greek New Testament](https://sites.google.com/site/nestle1904/): a public-domain critical edition (this is the 1904 Nestle text, not the later copyrighted Nestle-Aland editions). Publisher of the digital edition: the Nestle 1904 project site. Licence: the official site states that the work is in the public domain worldwide and is free of known copyright restrictions ([site](https://sites.google.com/site/nestle1904/)). Official downloads: [downloads page](https://sites.google.com/site/nestle1904/downloads).
- [SBL Greek New Testament](https://www.sblgnt.com/): a modern critical edition, free to read and download. Publisher: Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible Software, as stated on the site. Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International ([licence](https://www.sblgnt.com/license/); [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). Downloads: [SBLGNT download page](https://www.sblgnt.com/download/).
- [Robinson-Pierpont, Byzantine Textform](https://byzantinetext.com/study/editions/robinson-pierpont/): a public-domain Greek New Testament edition. Licence: the editors released the text into the public domain, with a request that their names, the title, and their disclaimer be retained in reproductions ([edition page](https://byzantinetext.com/study/editions/robinson-pierpont/)).
- [Codex Sinaiticus manuscript viewer](https://www.codexsinaiticus.org/en/manuscript.aspx): images of a fourth-century parchment manuscript of the Greek Bible, on the official project site. Publisher: the Codex Sinaiticus Project. Licence: see the official [copyright page](https://www.codexsinaiticus.org/en/copyright.aspx). That copyright page, and the home and viewer pages, is listed so the official terms can be read there. The exact wording is not quoted here because that page could not be retrieved as static text. No images are copied here.

The later Nestle-Aland (NA27/NA28) and UBS Greek New Testament editions are copyrighted and are not listed.
