# Cao Dai

Links only. No scripture text is copied here.

Cao Dai scriptures include collections of official religious texts in Vietnamese, often referred to under titles such as the Thánh Ngôn Hiệp Tuyển, together with other official writings.

- [Official Cao Dai English site](https://caodai.com.vn/en): an official public site of the Cao Dai Holy See, free to browse. Publisher, as the site's copyright line states: Hoi Thanh Cao Dai Toa Thanh Tay Ninh. Licence: the official English site identifies the copyright holder as Hoi Thanh Cao Dai Toa Thanh Tay Ninh.
