# Traditional African religions

Links only. No scripture text is copied here.

Traditional African religions are many, not one, and they are carried in large part by oral tradition, ritual and community teaching. There is no single shared written scripture for the continent.

Some older collections of oral narratives, praise poetry and ethnographic texts have been published and later placed in public-domain archives. Those collections are not a substitute for living oral tradition, and they should not be treated as a single African "canon."

- [Sacred Texts, Africa](https://sacred-texts.com/afr/index.htm): a free archive of such older published collections. Publisher: Internet Sacred Text Archive. Licence: the site states that most of its texts are freely available to read without an account; many texts are in the public domain, while the site's design and some commentary remain protected ([terms of service](https://sacred-texts.com/terms); [copyrights and public domain](https://sacred-texts.com/copyrights)).

No official, pan-African scripture is listed, because none exists.
