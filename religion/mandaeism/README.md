# Mandaeism

Links only. No scripture text is copied here.

The principal Mandaean scriptures include the Ginza Rabba, the Mandaean Book of John, and the Qolasta, in Mandaic. Academic translations of some of these works exist in older printed books, but a complete, free, official or clearly licensed online edition, with a confirmed public licence or terms page and no sign-in, was not found. Candidate pages that were tried either did not exist or did not respond.

There is therefore no scripture link on this page. That is a fact about what could be confirmed as free to use, not a substitute text.
