# Shinto — Kojiki and Nihon Shoki

Links only. No scripture text is copied here. The Kojiki and the Nihon Shoki (Nihongi) are the principal early written chronicles used in Shinto study, in Classical Japanese and Classical Chinese. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.
