# Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

Links only. No scripture text is copied here. The tradition's distinctive scripture includes the Book of Mormon, together with the Doctrine and Covenants and the Pearl of Great Price, and it also uses the Bible. Official texts are published by the Church in English and in many other languages. Each link names its source, publisher, and licence or terms as the site states them.

## Original language of the official published text (English, as officially issued)

The Book of Mormon was officially published in English. The Church's current official text is the one on its own site.

- [Official scriptures library](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures?lang=eng): the Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, Pearl of Great Price, and the Bible as the Church presents them. Publisher: The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Licence: the official terms of use govern access to the site and its scriptures; the terms restrict commercial use of the site and require that copyright and trademark notices be left in place ([terms of use](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/legal/terms-of-use)).
- [Book of Mormon, official English edition](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm?lang=eng): free to read with no sign-in. Publisher: The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Licence: the official terms of use govern access to the site and its scriptures; the terms restrict commercial use of the site and require that copyright and trademark notices be left in place ([terms of use](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/legal/terms-of-use)).
